How it works

Four steps, about a minute

  1. Set your route

    Search for a pickup and a destination, or tap them on the map.

  2. Check the fare

    The route is drawn on the map with its distance, time and price.

  3. Confirm the ride

    Add your phone number and departure time, then pick cash or card.

  4. Follow your taxi

    Watch it drive to the destination. The finished ride goes into your history.
