About this project

A simulator, not a taxi company

No real car will turn up and no money changes hands. Taxi App is a demo of a full booking experience, built with Angular and Leaflet on top of open map data.

  • Your data stays with you

    Rides are kept in your browser's local storage. There's no account and no server storing them.

  • No card details, ever

    Choosing card or cash only labels the ride. The app never asks for a card number.
